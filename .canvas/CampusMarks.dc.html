Every cell is locked and the save bar is gone. A closed term is read at, not written to; reopening it is a request, not a button.

Three columns carry marks, two are computed. Term total and position are never editable and never coloured — a figure a teacher cannot change should not look like a figure a teacher can change. Position recomputes across all 34 learners on save, not on keystroke, so the column does not flicker while a class is being typed in.
