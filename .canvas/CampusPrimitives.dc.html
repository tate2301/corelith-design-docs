Campus primitives

The school edition — one learner record, three portals, one register Sheet 21

Campus carries a learner from admission to leaving: registers, marks, report cards, timetables, fees in US$ and ZWG, staff and payroll, across a learner portal, a teacher portal and a guardian portal. It is the same system underneath, so it borrows every primitive it can — Identity, RecordChip, the status pills, the table. What follows is only what a school needs and a ledger does not. The module hue is #0e7490, the same value Stock carries; a school and a warehouse are never on screen together.

Collection is a switch, not a badge, because it is a setting a head of school changes at the desk and the gate reads an hour later. This is the one control in the system that takes colour when selected rather than the usual raised white chip. A register is read across a classroom at arm's length and the answer to "who is missing" has to survive that distance; three neutral chips do not. The order is fixed — present, absent, late — because muscle memory beats reading after the second week of term, and present is first because it is nine taps in ten. Absent and late each open one further field and no more: a reason, or a time.

The rate rounds down. 62 of 65 is 95.4%, and a school that prints 96% will lose the argument with the first guardian who does the division. A payment plan is amber, not green: the school has agreed to it and still has to watch it. Arrears is the only fee state that earns red, and it earns it with an age — US$ 285.00 at 34 days and US$ 285.00 at 4 days are different problems. The ZWG figure is a conversion at the day's rate carried beside the US$ amount; the account is kept in US$ and the rate is always printed with it.

Ready is amber and not green, because ready is the state where the work stops until a person moves it. Issued is the only green one and it carries who issued it and when — a report card is a document a guardian will quote back to the school two years later. The count is in the caption rather than inside the pill: "In progress" is the state and "2 of 3 in" is how far.

The flag sits on the staff record and on every list that shows it, and it does not hold the salary: payroll runs, the flag follows. A school that blocks a teacher's pay over an unsigned form loses the teacher, so the warning is loud and the consequence is administrative. Expired is red because the person is teaching without a contract today; unsigned is amber because there is a contract and it is waiting for a pen.
