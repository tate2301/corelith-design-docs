The identity column is pinned, so an account never scrolls away from its figures — the edge shadow is the only thing that says so. Group subtotals and the foot are computed from the whole result set, not the page: a total that changes when you turn the page is a total nobody trusts. The foot is sticky and carries the day’s ZWG line, because the number a person is asked about is the one in the other currency.
