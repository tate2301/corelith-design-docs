Touch, gloves, a counter, a queue. Forced by the shell — not offered as a preference the person can turn off.

A mode is one attribute on a container — data-d="compact". It cascades and it nests: a comfortable tender drawer inside a compact back-office shell is legal, and is how the register sits in the day-close screen. Note what did not move — pill, avatar, hairline and icon are identical in all three.

24 px floor

The dashed line. Everything a finger or a pointer can hit sits above it.

Every control, table row, list row and menu row clears 24 × 24 in every mode. The one exception is --h-xs at 20 and 22, which clears the floor through 2.5.8's spacing exception instead: an xs control is only legal in a row at least 24 tall with no other target within 24 of it. It is never the only route to an action — the same action sits on the row menu at --h-md.

Density is a measure of how much air a row is given, not a zoom. A 28px avatar in comfortable would make the three modes three different products, and a screenshot from one would stop being evidence about another.
