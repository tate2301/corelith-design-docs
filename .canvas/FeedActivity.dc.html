Machine-written events roll up: fourteen fiscalisations become one line with a count and a total, and the expander beneath restores them. Anything a person wrote — a note, an email, a comment — never rolls up, however many arrive together, because a summary of what somebody said is not what they said. Time sits at the end of the line and stays regular weight; it is the one thing on every row and would otherwise be the loudest.
