The rhythm — 4 and 8, nothing between
4 — inside a control
5 — label to field, field to hint
8 — between choices in a group
12 — between stacked fields
16 — between fields in a row
20 — between fieldsets

A form has exactly two horizontal rhythms and four vertical ones. Everything else — a stray 10, a hopeful 14 — is what makes a long form feel assembled rather than designed.
