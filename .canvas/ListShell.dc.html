Why the bar is built this way
Segments count the whole list, not the page

Each figure is computed across all 1,284 records, not the 50 on screen, so the bar doubles as the headline. Only the active segment is a white raised chip; the rest sit flat on the wash so the group reads as one control. Within limit, Over limit and Overdue partition the total exactly.

A segment with nothing in it stays visible

Closed off shows a zero at half opacity rather than disappearing. A filter bar whose shape changes under you is a filter bar nobody trusts.

Rows, selection, loading
Identity carries the first column

Companies take initials on their module hue, sole traders take a face — both at 24px over the account number, so one rhythm holds the column. Hover washes, selection is accent-soft, focus rings the whole row, and a closed-off account drops to muted with its tick disabled and a lock for the chevron.

Selection floats, the next page appends

The action bar rides over the rows it acts on rather than replacing the toolbar, so filters and selection stay legible together; past three verbs, the chevron. The next page arrives as skeletons at the foot, so a slow FDMS call never makes read rows jump.
