Touch first, density second

Comfortable is the phone's density: 44px controls, 44px filter chips, 72px rows, a 64px tab bar. Nothing a thumb can reach is smaller than 44px, and the row keeps a 40px avatar so a name is recognised before it is read.

The filter bar is the desktop bar, scrolled

Same segments, same counts, same white raised chip for the active one — it simply scrolls instead of wrapping, and fades at the edge so the fourth segment is visibly there. Swipe on a row reveals two verbs; the rest live on the record.

The record leads with identity and money

64px avatar, name, account number, then the three figures a credit controller phones about. The utilisation bar sits under them rather than beside, because at 390px a bar and a figure cannot share a line without one of them shrinking.

Tabs scroll, actions are pinned

The strip fades at the right so the sixth tab is known to exist; the underline, not a chip, marks the current one. The foot bar never scrolls away — one filled verb, one plain, and everything else behind the chevron. No status bar and no keyboard are drawn: that space belongs to the phone, not to us.
