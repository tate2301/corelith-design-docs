The rail names the module and never changes. The sidebar names the section and changes with it. An active item is a white pill on the wash — a border, a 2px accent bar and a step up in surface, not a block of accent colour that fights everything beside it.

Sub-items hang off a single hairline guide rather than off indentation alone, so the eye can find the parent from four rows down without counting pixels.
