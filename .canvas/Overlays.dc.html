Focus. A tooltip holds no focus at all. It appears on hover after 300ms and instantly on keyboard focus, and vanishes on blur or Escape. Twelve surfaces, one rule read three ways: whatever opens takes focus only if it also traps it; whatever traps focus dims the page behind it; and whatever closes hands focus back to the exact control that opened it. Everything that does not trap — tooltip, hover card, toast — must never move focus at all, because a person mid-sentence in a journal line cannot be interrupted by a notice.
