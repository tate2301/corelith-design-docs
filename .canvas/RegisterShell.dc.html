Why the screen is shaped like this

Held and queued are not the same word. Four sales are held — parked for a customer who went back for something. Nine are queued — final on this terminal, waiting only for the line.

Three of the four postings are local and final the moment the sale ends; only the fiscal signature needs ZIMRA, so it queues and the receipt prints marked pending.

Every row a finger touches is at least 44px, off var(--row) and var(--h-lg) — basket lines, keypad keys, tenders, the split legs, the rate strip and the four postings, each of which opens on its chevron. Two things sit below that height and neither takes a tap: the panel captions at 26px and the quantity chip on a basket line at 32px, which is a readout inside a 44px row. A mis-hit at a till is a void and a fiscal reversal, watched by a queue.
