The rule these all follow

A sparse page is left sparse. Where there is one figure to show we show one figure, at full size, on a page that is mostly ground — we do not add a second chart, a tip card or a carousel of things a person did not ask for to make the screen look busy. Emptiness is information about the business.

Every one of these states is reachable on a normal Tuesday in Msasa: the line drops, a till queues, August closes, two people edit the same account. None of them is an exception, so none of them is left to a browser default or a spinner.

Colour is spent only where somebody must act. Queued, Closed off and Not started stay neutral. Amber means a person should look today; red means a figure is wrong or a change was refused. Nothing else earns a hue.
