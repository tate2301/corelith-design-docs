The status vocabulary — fixed, closed, and not ours to extend
Posted Signed Reconciled Matched Filed Final Issued
Queued In progress Trading Prepared Ready
Needs approval Unmatched Overdue Failed
Draft Open Closed off Not started

Twenty words, five tones. Ready and Issued are the Campus report-card states — a card is Ready once marks are in and signed off, and Issued once it has gone to the guardian — and they sit in this same ledger, not a Campus one of their own. A new state needs a new word from the ledger, not a new colour — if the accountant does not say it out loud, it does not get a pill.

Rules

Colour on a figure means one of two things only: negative, or past a threshold. A large number is not coloured for being large.

Every figure primitive renders a null as an em dash. A blank cell is indistinguishable from a failed fetch, and in a ledger that difference is the whole job.

Thresholds live on the record — the credit limit, the minimum cover, the ageing band. The component reads them; it never carries a default of its own.
