Two sizes only — type.ui at 13 and type.micro at 11. Four levels of hierarchy still read, and every one of them is made from something other than size.

micro, subtleNames the region. Reads last, and only when someone is looking for it.
ui 600, inkThe one thing the card is about — the account, and the figure owed.
ui 400, inkValues. Same size as their labels, separated only by weight and mono.
ui 400, mutedLabels. Present, addressable, and out of the way.
pill, criticalThe only colour spent, on the only state that needs a person.

A size step is a blunt instrument: it costs vertical space, it changes the rhythm of the row, and it can only be used a few times before the page stops having a loudest element. Weight and colour cost nothing and can be used everywhere.

700 was removed from the loaded family, not merely discouraged. At 13px on a hairline surface Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at 700 closes its counters and blooms against a 1px line; the extra emphasis it buys is emphasis the layout should not have needed. If 600 is not enough, the problem is the amount of content next to it.

Measure — 65 characters
65ch

Every receipt is signed by the ZIMRA fiscal device before it prints. If the device is offline the sale completes anyway and the receipt is queued; Corelith signs and files it the moment the device answers. The cashier is told only that the queue is holding.

Prose is capped at 65 characters. Past that the return sweep starts to miss, and a support notice becomes something a cashier skips. .note caps itself at 64ch for the same reason.

Three sizes. The total is the largest thing on the card, and it is the only thing anyone opens a cash-up to see.
Seven sizes, each a step from the last. The rows now form a staircase, "Cash-up" competes with the total, and the tender names read as five different kinds of thing when they are one kind of thing.

Both cards use all the steps the system has. The right one is worse for exactly that reason: when the ramp is spent on every distinction, nothing is left to mark the one that matters. A step is a scarce resource — the seven exist so that a view can pick three.
