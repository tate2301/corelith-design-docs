Lists kit

Six list shapes for six kinds of data.

The right list for a given data type is rarely a table. Projects need members and progress. Listings need imagery. Invoices group by date. People want avatar + role + last seen. Activity wants a timeline. Records want a definition list. Pick by content, not by habit.
