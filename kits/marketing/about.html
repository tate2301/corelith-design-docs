About

We're building the ERP we wanted to use.

Corelith started in a Harare back office in 2023, after one too many month-ends spent reconciling spreadsheets that should have agreed.

Most ERPs are built for finance directors at companies with 200 staff. Most small businesses don't have a finance director — they have an owner who also runs sales, and a bookkeeper who comes in on Tuesdays. The tools they're given are either too small (a spreadsheet that becomes a graveyard) or too big (six modules, four implementations consultants, no time saved).

We thought there was room for a third option: a back office that's serious about the books but feels like a tool, not a project. One ledger underneath everything, so the numbers always agree. A hyperlegible interface, because operators stare at it eight hours a day. Pricing a small business can afford without negotiating.

That's Corelith. We're still small, and that's the point — we ship what real operators ask for, not what big-enterprise procurement check-lists demand.

Principles

How we build.

Four commitments we make to every team that picks Corelith.

One ledger

Every module posts into one source of truth. No sync jobs, no reconciliation tax.

Your data is yours

Full SQL export at any time, in any tier. Nothing proprietary, nothing held hostage.

Hyperlegible

Built for the eyes that look at it all day. No micro-type, no decorative fluff.

Reversible

Every posted entry is signed, timestamped, and reversible. Mistakes don't become incidents.
