Features

Every module, built on one ledger.

Corelith is a single workspace for the back office. Six modules that all share one source of truth.

Sales & POS

From shop-floor till to B2B quote-to-cash, every sale lives in the same pipeline. Cash, card, mobile money, account terms — all reconciled in real time.

  • Touch-friendly POS with offline mode
  • Quote → order → invoice → receipt, no re-keying
  • Per-customer pricing, terms, and credit limits
  • Real-time GP margin per line, per till, per branch

See the POS recipe →

Stock & warehouse

Track every SKU across every location. Bin-level visibility, batch and serial tracking, and live cost roll-up for COGS that always agrees with the GL.

  • Multi-location stock with one-step transfers
  • Cycle counts that don't stop the day
  • FIFO, average, or standard costing — your call
  • Restock signals driven by real sell-through

Open the warehouse demo →

General ledger

A double-entry GL that the rest of the system actually posts into. No more sync jobs, no more reconciliation spreadsheets at month-end.

  • Configurable chart with sub-accounts and dimensions
  • Multi-entity, multi-currency, with consolidation
  • Period close as a one-click checklist
  • Every report drills to the originating transaction

Read the GL guide →

Payroll & HR

Run payroll in the language your team reads. Pre-built tax tables for the region, leave and loan accruals already posted, EFT export ready for the bank.

  • Local statutory deductions configured out of the box
  • Self-service for time, leave, and payslips
  • Pay slips in Shona, Ndebele, or English
  • One-click bulk EFT to your bank

Try the payroll demo →

Reporting

Trial balance, P&L, cash flow, and dashboards that recompute as transactions post. No "data refresh" wait, no "as-of yesterday" disclaimers.

  • Live dashboards for owners, ops, and finance
  • Comparative reports across periods and entities
  • Scheduled email digests for the team
  • CSV, XLSX, PDF exports — every report, every time

See report patterns →

Admin & security

Role-based access, every change signed, every login logged. Built for the auditor as much as for the operator.

  • Granular roles down to the action level
  • SSO via Google, Microsoft, or SAML
  • Immutable audit log of every posted entry
  • Full SQL export — your data is always yours

Explore admin tools →
