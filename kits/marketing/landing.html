Questions

Things people ask before they sign up.

Do I need to migrate my data to get started?

No. You can start fresh on Corelith and bring history in later, or import via CSV from your old system at any time. Our import ledger keeps every batch reversible.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The POS and warehouse modules keep working when the link drops. Postings reconcile automatically when you're back online.

Which countries do you handle tax for?

Zimbabwe, South Africa, Botswana, Zambia, and Kenya are pre-configured. Other countries run with a custom tax table — we'll help you set it up.

Can my accountant log in?

Absolutely. Auditor seats are free on Team and Owner tiers. They get read-only access scoped to whichever entities you choose.

What if I outgrow it?

The ledger is yours — full SQL export at any time, no proprietary formats. We've never lost a customer to "couldn't get my data out."

Is there a free trial on paid tiers?

Yes, 30 days on Team and Owner, no card required. Drop down to Starter at any point if you decide it's not for you.
