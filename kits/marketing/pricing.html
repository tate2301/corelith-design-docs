Pricing FAQ

Billing, currencies, trials.

How does billing work?

Monthly or annual, billed in USD. Annual gets two months free. We don't auto-charge on overages — if you outgrow a tier, we email you and you decide.

Is there a free trial on paid tiers?

Yes. Team and Owner come with a 30-day trial, no card required. You can drop down to Starter at any point if it's not for you.

Can I pay in local currency?

Yes. We accept ZWL, ZAR, KES, BWP, ZMW, USD, and EUR. Invoices show your local currency with the day's interbank rate. We won't pad with FX margin.
