Six portals · purpose-built for each audience

Each portal is its own shell — chrome stripped to what its audience needs and the device it lives on. POS for cashiers, Parent for guardians, Student for learners, Teacher for the classroom, Staff for back-office, Admin for the platform team. All six ship as web, PWA, and packaged mobile apps.
