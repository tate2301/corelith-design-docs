Nine portals · purpose-built for each audience

Each portal is its own shell — chrome stripped to what its audience needs and the device it lives on. POS for cashiers, Parent for guardians, Student for learners, Teacher for the classroom, Staff for back-office, Admin for the platform team, plus the three new operator portals: Scrap Yard, Gold Mine Clerk, and Owner / Manager. Every portal ships as a fully-interactive frontend-only app you can drive in this tab.
