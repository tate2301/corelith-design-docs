Nine apps · one for each person in your business

Every person in a small business needs different things. The cashier needs a quick till. The parent needs a fees balance. The shop owner needs to know if money came in today. So we built nine apps — one for each — and gave each one only what its user actually uses. They all work on a phone.

Try the live app or read about it first

Two ways to look around: Open app opens the working app right here in your browser — sign in, click around, your work is saved as you go. View hub opens a short page explaining what's inside. Pick whichever you prefer.

POS terminal

/portals/pos

For cashiers and field buyers. Ring up a sale, take payment, print the receipt. Refund with a manager PIN. Cash-up at the end of the shift and see if the till's short or over. Takes cash, card, EcoCash, OneMoney — or split between them.

Parent portal

/portals/parent

For parents and guardians. See what fees you still owe, when your child was at school, their marks, and any school news. Switch between your kids in one tap. Pay fees by EcoCash, OneMoney, bank transfer or card. Works well even on slow internet.

Student portal

/portals/student

For students. See your timetable, today's classes, your marks, and homework you still have to hand in. Borrow and return library books by scanning them. Set goals and tick them off. Nothing extra — just the stuff you actually need each day.

Teacher portal

/portals/teacher

For teachers. Mark attendance from your phone, enter marks on a tablet, plan your next lesson, message a parent. Best on a tablet in class; on a computer when you sit down to mark; on your phone when you're moving around the school.

Staff portal

/portals/staff

For everyone who works at the business. See your payslip with PAYE, NSSA and other deductions broken down. Ask for leave (and approve your team's leave if you're a manager). Clock in and out from your phone. Claim back money you spent for work. Find a workmate's number.

Admin

/portals/admin

For whoever runs the system day-to-day. Add or remove users and pick what each one can see. Check who did what (history of changes). See your bill and what you're paying for. Connect to other tools (Stripe, EcoCash, NMB, Slack and so on). Back up your data.

Scrap Yard Operator New

/portals/scrap

For people working at a scrap-metal yard. Open the day and check the scale is right. Weigh each load coming in, pick the material, take a photo, save the supplier's details. Pay suppliers by EcoCash, OneMoney, bank or cash. Send big loads off to the buyer (e.g. Cape Steel via Beitbridge).

Gold Mine Clerk New

/portals/gold

For clerks at gold mines (Bindura, Mukaradzi, Mazowe River). Record each pour into the same ledger the mine already uses — Gross, Diesel, Shoots, LCD, Workers, Company, Balance. Send batches to the refinery, track payments coming back, watch the 25% RBZ retention, and export the ZIMRA / MMCZ forms.

Owner / Manager New

/portals/owner · dark default

For the owner running the whole business. See today's numbers across every shop, mine and yard. Compare branches. See how much cash is sitting in each bank account and EcoCash float. Who owes you, who you owe. Profit by line of business. What's coming in the next 12 weeks. Plus a list of things that need your eyes today, and reminders for ZIMRA, NSSA and NEC due dates.

Each app works on its own — no extra setup

Open any app in this browser, sign in, and your work is saved as you go. Close the tab and come back later — your sale, your draft pour, your half-filled timesheet — it's all still there. This is exactly the experience your team will see in production.
