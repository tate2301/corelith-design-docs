Run the whole business from your phone

One screen for the owner of a small or medium business — a hardware shop, a salon chain, a courier service, a restaurant group, a workshop, whatever you run. Cash on hand, sales today, money owed to you, money you owe, things to look at, tax & licence dates. Dark screen by default. Mobile first — built to check in five minutes on the way to work, then sit with for an hour at the desk.

Shells

Mobile first · scales to the desk

Designed for a phone first. Cards stack in a single column with big tap targets; the four primary tools (Today, Money, Flags, More) sit in a bottom tab bar. On a tablet, the cards grow side by side. On a desktop the bottom tabs swap for a left sidebar so you can sit with it all morning.

Mobile primary

Bottom tab bar on phones (Today · Money · Flags · More) · single-column cards with 44 px tap targets · row taps open as bottom sheets · charts swap to plain-text lists on phones · left sidebar appears at 1024 px and up.
