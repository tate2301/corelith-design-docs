Scrap Yard Operator — weigh, record, pay

A focused mobile-first prototype for the clerk standing next to the weighbridge. Calibrate the scale to open the day, weigh loads of copper / aluminium / brass / steel / lead / stainless, then pay suppliers by EcoCash, OneMoney, cash or bank.
