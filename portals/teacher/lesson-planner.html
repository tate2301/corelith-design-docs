Linear equations · setting up word problems

Form 2A · Mathematics · Mr Chari · Room 12
ZIMSEC 4004 Unit 7 · L7.2 CALA cycle 2 Ready
Topic
Translating real-life word problems into linear equations using one unknown. Focus on Zimbabwean contexts — kombi fares, mealie-meal pricing, school tuck-shop change.
Learning objectives
  • Identify the unknown and assign a variable (let x = …).
  • Translate phrases like "three times as much", "half of", "less than" into algebraic expressions.
  • Form and solve a single linear equation in one variable.
  • Check the solution against the original word problem context.
