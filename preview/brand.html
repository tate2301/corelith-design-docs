Five principles

When in doubt, read the line out loud. If it sounds like marketing, rewrite it.

01

Name the real noun

Say "invoice", "site", "shift", "settlement". Never "item", "record", "entity".

02

Lead with the next action

Confirmations say what happened, then what to do next. Errors say what failed, then how to recover.

03

Trust the operator

Don't hand-hold. Don't apologize. If a feature is gated, say what's required — not "Please contact your administrator."

04

One sentence is plenty

The product is dense; the writing should be light. If a paragraph is needed, the design is wrong.

05

Quiet is correct

No exclamation points. No emoji. No celebratory copy. The work is the reward.

06

Use the canonical labels

Status vocabulary is fixed: Needs input · Running · Completed · Idle. Don't invent "Almost done" or "Stuck".

Writing in the wild

Real strings from real screens. Each pair shows the same intent, written two ways.

Do · button label
Log out
Verb. Two words max. No "now", no "please".
Don't
Sign me out of this device now
A button is not a sentence.
Do · empty state
No invoices yet. The first one shows up here once you bill a customer.
Says what's missing, then what makes it appear.
Don't
Nothing here yet! ✨ Get started by creating your first record.
Emoji, vague noun, ambient cheer.
Do · destructive confirm
Cancel plan? You'll keep Max access until Jun 8. After that, you'll be on the free plan.
Names the action, the end date, and what comes next.
Don't
Are you sure you want to cancel? This action cannot be undone.
No date, no "what next". "Cannot be undone" is technically true and operationally useless.
Do · field help
Claude will keep these in mind across chats and Cowork within Anthropic's guidelines.
Direct second-person. States the scope and the limit in one breath.
Don't
Tell us a bit about yourself so we can personalize your experience!
"A bit." "Personalize your experience." Marketing-speak in a settings form.
Do · session row
Needs input   Start ErrandHub peer-to-peer marketplace project
Status dot. The session's actual title. No extra adjectives.
Don't
⚠️ This conversation requires your attention — please review the latest message
Icon as scolding, "please" as filler, 13 words for what one dot does.
Do · billing line
Your subscription will auto renew on Jun 8, 2026.
A date in bold. That's the whole sentence.
Don't
Don't worry! Your account will be automatically renewed on the next billing cycle.
If the user has to be told not to worry, you've already failed.

Toasts & notifications

One line for the fact. One line, if needed, for the consequence. Never two paragraphs.
