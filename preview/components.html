Component library

Primitives, compounds, and the things they compose.

Three tiers. Primitives are atoms — a label, a button, a chip. Compounds combine atoms — a field, an alert, a chip group. Patterns combine compounds into reusable surfaces — a data toolbar, a settings row, a stat card.

How does Corelith handle multi-site reconciliation?
Every site is reconciled independently against its bank feed and POS daily total. Variances above policy tolerance open an incident that follows your approval workflow.
Can I edit a committed journal entry?
No. Committed entries are immutable. To correct them, post a reversing entry. The audit trail keeps both records.
How long are CSV imports retained?
Source files are retained for 7 years on the Operations and Enterprise plans, 30 days on Starter.
