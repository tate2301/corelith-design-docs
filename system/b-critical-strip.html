Do & don't

Do

Only show when there is something the operator must act on this shift.

Don't

Use it as a general "what changed". That belongs in a notifications popover.

Do

Tone matches highest severity. One danger turns the whole strip red.

Don't

Stack more than four items. If there are five, link to a queue.
