Do & don't

Do

Personalise to the operator’s role. A bursar sees fees; a cashier sees POS.

Don't

Repeat the sidebar nav. Quick links are about the operator’s next move.

Do

Lead each tile with a verb — "New sale", not "Sales".

Don't

Have more than 6 quick links visible. Past 6, the user picks slower than from the sidebar.
