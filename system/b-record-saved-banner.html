Record saved banner

Confirms an inline edit has been persisted. Sits above the field, fades after 3 seconds.

Stable .saved-banner Persistent toastField-level

Default

Saved · 12 seconds ago
Saved indicator

Do & don't

Do

Show "Saved · N seconds ago" inline so the operator knows their edit took.

Don't

Use a toast for inline edits. Toasts compete; inline confirms are quieter.

Do

Time-stamp from the save, not page load. The operator may have multiple tabs open.

Don't

Show "Saved" forever. After 60 s, fade to nothing.
