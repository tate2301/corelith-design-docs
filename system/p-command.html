Do & don't

Do

Index everything the operator searches by name — records, settings, actions.

Don't

Hide unique actions behind submenus inside the palette.

Do

Score recent and frequent items higher. The operator should land on what they’ve been using.

Don't

Auto-open the palette on page load. Always require a key press.
