Do & don't

Do

Parse free typing in the input. "3 jun" or "06/03/26" should both resolve.

Don't

Open the picker on focus. Click to open; focus to type.

Do

Show preset ranges in a sidebar — "Today", "This week", "Last 30 days".

Don't

Allow selecting dates outside the workspace fiscal range.
