Tonal hints

Do & don't

Do

Right-align. Mono font. Tabular numerals. Always.

Don't

Mix mono and proportional digits in the same column.

Do

Use the tonal color sparingly — only for the delta column, not the absolute number.

Don't

Strip trailing zeros. "$ 12" reads differently from "$ 12.00" in a money column.
