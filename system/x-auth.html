Shared rules

Do

Lead the primary action with a verb — "Sign in", not "Submit".

Don't

Show 8 different sign-in options. Email + one SSO is plenty.

Do

State the workspace name once the user lands.

Don't

Send the operator to a generic error page on bad credentials. Show the error inline.

Composition

Auth shell composes a split-pane layout — brand on the left, sign-in card on the right. Standalone surface; does not nest inside app shell.

Don't use this for: the in-app account-switcher (use role gate + a menu instead). For 2FA challenges from inside the app, use modal.

Responsive behaviour

ViewportBehaviour
Phone (≤ 720 px)Single column. Brand collapses to a centred logo at the top; card fills below.
Tablet (720 – 1023 px)Single column with a small brand strip at the top.
Desktop (≥ 1024 px)Split-pane: brand panel left (380 – 480 px), card right (centred).

Keyboard & accessibility

KeyBehaviour
TabMove through inputs in DOM order; primary CTA is the last stop.
EnterSubmit the form.
Esc (on 2FA / mention picker)Close the auxiliary surface; keep the parent form.
  • Page title is a single H1 — "Sign in", "Verify it's you", etc.
  • Inline errors render with role="alert" next to the offending field.
  • SSO buttons carry an explicit provider label; the icon alone is not an accessible name.
