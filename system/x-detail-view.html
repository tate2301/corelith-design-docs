Shared rules

What every composition obeys.

Do

Surface the next likely action in the hero. A student leads with "Record payment"; a receipt leads with "Refund".

Don't

Repeat hero data in the sidebar. Aside is for things not shown above.

Do

Anchor activity to the right column. The operator scans "what happened" while looking at "what is".

Don't

Cram too many sections. If a record has 10 subsystems, use detail tabs.
