Gold operations

Buying offices, processing operations, audit-grade settlement. Track every gram from production through settlement with ZIMRA-ready records.

Vertical kit 6 modules 6 pages Beta /verticals/gold
Modules

What this kit covers

Six connected modules. Every gram is traceable from gate to settlement.

01
Gold pours
Daily pour records — weights, melt loss, fineness assays, batch numbers.
02
Dispatch & receipt
Outbound shipments to refineries and inbound receipts with chain-of-custody.
03
Settlement control
Refinery returns, fineness reconciliation, price-on-day calculation.
04
Reconciliation
Daily three-way reconcile: pours, dispatches, settlements.
05
Exception alerts
Variance, fineness shortfall, late settlement, missing dispatch.
06
ZIMRA compliance
Statutory reports, electronic submission, audit-trail export.
