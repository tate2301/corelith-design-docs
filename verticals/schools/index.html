Schools / Campus

Student records, fees, attendance, timetables, results, parent portal. Six connected modules for Zimbabwe schools — admin, teacher, parent and student portals all anchored to the same student record.

Vertical kit 6 modules 6 pages Production /verticals/schools
Modules

What this kit covers

Six modules. The admin desktop side; portals are documented separately under Portals.

01
Student records
Roll, demographics, contacts, medical, history, photo.
02
Fee management
Invoices, balances, payment plans, scholarships, reminders.
03
Attendance
Daily roll, term summaries, late & absence reasons.
04
Timetables
Class, teacher, room — clash detection, publish to portals.
05
Results & reports
Marks entry, term reports, position in class, parent share.
06
Parent portal
Fee balances, attendance, results, notices — documented under Portals.
