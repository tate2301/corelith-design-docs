Scrap & recycling

Yard intake, weighing, grading, pricing, bulk sales. Buying discipline at the gate; cleaner trading downstream. The full intake-to-payout loop.

Vertical kit 6 modules 6 pages Production /verticals/scrap
Modules

What this kit covers

Six modules from gate to ledger.

01
Buying records
Supplier intake — weights, grades, prices, KYC documents.
02
Yard stock
Live stockpile balances by grade across every yard.
03
Pricing history
Daily buying prices by grade; margin against bulk sale price.
04
Bulk sales
Outbound contracts, weighbridge tickets, settlement.
05
Supplier history
Supplier ledger, volumes, average prices, dispute log.
06
Settlement control
Payouts — cash, EFT, mobile money — with reconciliation.
