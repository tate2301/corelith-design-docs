Thrift & secondhand

Bale intake, grading & sorting, lot inventory, sales tracking, supplier analysis. The thrift workflow from bale arrival to resale and margin analysis.

Vertical kit 5 modules 3 pages Beta /verticals/thrift
Modules

What this kit covers

Five modules from bale to lot to sale.

01
Bale intake
Bale weight, supplier, country of origin, category, photos.
02
Grading & sorting
Open a bale, grade contents into A / B / C lots; weight loss.
03
Lot inventory
Lots by grade and category with price-ladder by week.
04
Sales tracking
Daily sales by lot, basket size, sell-through speed.
05
Supplier analysis
Average margin by supplier and origin; reorder candidates.
